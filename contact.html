FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer dine-in?

No. We are a takeout and pickup kitchen only. This lets us focus entirely on food quality and fast preparation.

Do you deliver?

We do not offer direct delivery at this time. However, you may find us on third-party delivery apps in the Orlando area.

Can I place a large order?

Yes. For orders of 10+ items, please call at least 2 hours ahead so our kitchen can prepare everything on time.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Absolutely. Many of our Thai, Chinese, and sushi dishes have vegetarian variations. Just ask when ordering.
