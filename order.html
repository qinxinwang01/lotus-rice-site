Takeout & Pickup

How to Order

We're a takeout-only kitchen. No reservations, no dine-in — just great food, ready when you are.

3 Simple Steps

Ordering Is Easy

1

Browse the Menu

Check out our full menu online. We offer Thai, Chinese, Japanese, and a selection of drinks and alcohol.

2

Place Your Order

Call us at (407) 555-0188 or order directly through our website — whichever is easier for you. We'll have it ready when you arrive.

3

Pick It Up

Head to our location in Windermere and grab your order from the pickup counter. Quick, simple, and fresh.

Good to Know

Pickup Details

Walk-In Orders

You're welcome to walk in and order at the counter. Wait times vary depending on how busy the kitchen is — calling ahead is recommended for the fastest experience.

Large & Group Orders

Ordering for a group or event? Call us at least 2 hours in advance for orders of 10 or more items so we can have everything ready on time.

Payment

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, Apple Pay, and cash. Payment is collected at pickup.

Allergies & Dietary Needs

Please let us know about any food allergies when you call. Many dishes can be adjusted for dietary needs, including vegetarian and gluten-free options.
